# Reports and revision outputs

This folder contains outputs generated by the notebooks and revision-analysis scripts.

## Original recovered reports

- `adc_data_report.pdf`
- `adc_platform_report.pdf`

These were recovered from the original project and are retained for provenance.

## Revision-analysis outputs

- `rigorous_model_audit/`  
  Repeated/nested cross-validation, learning curves, feature-importance stability, and noise-sensitivity outputs.

- `metric_quality_audit/`  
  Metrics beyond accuracy, per-class summaries, normalized confusion matrices, probability metrics, and hold-out seed sensitivity.

- `platform_group_validation/`  
  Leave-one-named-platform-out analyses.

- `validation_20_audit/`  
  Constrained 20-ADC literature stress-test analyses and feature-range checks.

- `revision_analysis/`  
  Feature-ablation, collinearity, hold-out uncertainty, and model/literature compatibility outputs.

- `figures/`  
  Revised manuscript-support figures derived from the revision analyses.

## Interpretation

These reports are intended to document the behavior of the synthetic benchmark and the limits of transfer to incomplete literature-derived records. They do not establish external validation of the trained model.
